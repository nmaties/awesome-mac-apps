## Add your app

Apps are added on Mac Browse, not through pull requests. [Submit yours](https://macbrowse.com/submit?ref=github) in a few minutes, on the free plan or a paid one, and it appears here the day after it is approved. For fixes and removals, see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
